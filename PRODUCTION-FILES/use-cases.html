The Challenge

Sites are expected to be ready.
But the knowledge they need is everywhere except where they work.

01
Protocol overload at activation
Sites manage complex protocols, amendments, SOPs, training requirements, and multiple sponsor systems simultaneously — before a single patient is enrolled.
02
Login fatigue and information gaps
Site teams work across portals, emails, binders, and disconnected study documents — creating fragmented access and preventable knowledge gaps.
03
Training ≠ readiness
Completing a training module does not mean a coordinator is ready to answer a live protocol question. The gap between checked-off and truly prepared costs trials dearly.
04
Protocol changes drive deviations
Version confusion, amendment delays, and slow access to updated information increase the risk of preventable, information-driven protocol deviations.
05
Slow staff ramp-up
New coordinators and site staff take longer to become effective because study knowledge is scattered across documents, systems, and people — not embedded where they work.
